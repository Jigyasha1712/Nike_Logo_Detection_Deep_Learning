VGG16 Transfer Learning Architecture

02. Pre-trained Base VGG16 ImageNet convolutional base with frozen early layer weights.
03. Custom Dense Head GlobalAveragePooling2D + Dense layers with Sigmoid activation.
04. Binary Output Logo Presence vs Non-Logo background classification.
